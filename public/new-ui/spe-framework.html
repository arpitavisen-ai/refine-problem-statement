The shift

The traditional lifecycle asks: how can we deliver this idea efficiently?

This framework asks

How can we prove this idea is worth delivering — before we build it?

Success is how early a weak idea dies, not how many ideas reach delivery. The aim is to fail earlier, not more often — when the cost of being wrong is lowest.

Phase 01 of 06 · Strategy

AI does not own the lifecycle. People do. Every phase names the decision a human owns, and the pipeline will not promote without it.

Why we keep a learning log
Not a record of failure
An idea that failed on cost or technology this year may be viable next year. The log is how we will know it is worth revisiting.
Organisational memory
Before a new case tests an assumption, the log is searched. Teams stop quietly re-running each other's experiments.
Success is a kill rate
Measured by how quickly a weak idea is eliminated, not by how many ideas reach delivery.

Design spans phases 3 to 4, Delivery 4 to 5, Validation 5 to 6, and Operations phase 6. The learning log spans all six phases. Five named stage gates control the handoffs: Gate 0 Discovery ready before Discovery, Gate 1 Discover and Validate before Design, Gate 2 Ready to Build before Delivery, Gate 3 Release Ready before Operations, and Gate 4 Value Realised inside Operations, looping back to Discovery. Three loops also return to Discovery: from Design, from Validation, and from Operations.

Bars extend only as far as you have walked. Discovery never closes; the gates control each handoff, and the loops are where evidence re-enters it.

Phase 01 of 06

Strategy

Define the why, what, and who before building anything.

What this phase delivers

AI sharpens strategic foresight and investment confidence. Humans own the vision, judgement, and accountability.
What we deliverHow S&PE differentiates

A dated, sourced competitor and policy map — dozens of sources read in days, every claim traceable, and the asset reusable on your next business case rather than binned with the deck. You decide which segment you play in and what you refuse to build.

Most AI-advisory work stops at a slide of trends. We tie the scan to a phase-01 gate decision, so what lands on your desk is a decision input your investment board can attack, with the sources attached to defend it.

A written data position before a penny of build spend: what the dataset actually contains, who holds it, on what legal terms — reachable, restricted, or not there at all.

Most programmes discover the data gap mid-build, when the fix is a re-plan and a credibility problem. We interrogate data reality before scoping, and we will tell you the idea is dead at phase 01. Killing a bad idea here is the cheapest money you will spend with us.

A measurable objective set — north star plus input drivers — written as a commitment the delivery team is held to at every later gate. You decide which metric the business actually signs up to.

Workshop aspirations don't survive contact with delivery. We make the north star testable and then score our own build against it in validation, reporting the gap without smoothing it. The metric follows the work instead of decorating the kick-off.

Case 01 · NHS
We believed
National feedback data would give us comment-level text through a national interface.
We tested
Interrogated the national dataset, its submission routes and its publication cadence.
We learned
It is aggregate counts with no free text. Comment data is held locally by each trust.
So we changed

Phase 02 of 06 · The engine

Discovery

The engine of the lifecycle rather than a stage in it. Hypotheses are generated, tested and killed here — and discovery keeps running underneath every phase that follows.

What this phase delivers

Persona simulation · blueprint generation · assumption ranking
What we deliverHow S&PE differentiates

A research-derived persona pack plus simulated review transcripts, in hours not weeks — so a concept gets challenged before it gets funded. You decide which persona the first release serves and which one waits.

Traditional discovery spends its budget recruiting participants for a concept that may not survive the first round. We run the concept against evidence-built personas first and spend your research money on the questions that are still open.

A service blueprint staged end to end with every AI-augmented step marked and the automation boundary drawn explicitly — the document your operational and risk stakeholders can actually sign.

"AI-powered" is where most proposals stop. We show, per step, where automation ends and staff judgement starts. That single drawing is what gets an AI product through operational governance rather than back to the drawing board.

A hypothesis log with a confidence level against every item and the cheapest test that would settle each one. You decide which assumption gets tested now and which you knowingly carry.

Roadmaps are usually a list of features with no stated risk. Ours is a list of beliefs ranked by what it costs to be wrong about them — which is how scope gets cut on evidence rather than on budget panic.

Phase 03 of 06

Design

Treat every iteration as an experiment rather than a deliverable, built on the client's own design system so what leadership sees is what engineering can ship.

What this phase delivers

Prototype generation · design-system critique · content design
What we deliverHow S&PE differentiates

Working, clickable prototypes built on your real design tokens and components — testable with real users the same day, not a static mockup that has to be rebuilt to be believed. You decide whether the idea is worth putting in front of anyone.

Most design phases hand over flat comps and discover the gap between the picture and the platform during the build. Because we prototype on your own system, what stakeholders approve is what engineering inherits.

An issue list against your design system and your accessibility standard, with specific WCAG references, raised during design rather than at audit.

Accessibility remediation after launch is the most expensive kind of rework and, in regulated sectors, the kind that carries a public finding. We treat AA as the build floor and evidence it per component, so your audit is a confirmation rather than a discovery.

Interface content written in your house voice — reading level, tone and conventions applied consistently across every surface. You sign off any wording that carries clinical, legal or financial risk.

Content design is usually the thing nobody budgeted for, so it arrives late and off-brand. We draft it with the interface and route the risky sentences to the people accountable for them.

Phase 04 of 06

Delivery

Build the hypothesis that was agreed, and nothing that arrived without evidence. The backlog behaves like a portfolio of hypotheses rather than a queue of work.

What this phase delivers

Code generation · self-review against logs · decision capture
What we deliverHow S&PE differentiates

Working software, not a prototype hand-off: scaffolded components, the repository and the deployment pipeline, with tests written alongside the code. Your architects set the boundaries and decide what gets merged.

AI coding speed usually evaporates in review. We generate from reviewed build prompts inside architectural boundaries a human has set, so the gain shows up in merged, tested code rather than in a branch someone quietly rewrites later.

Every deviation from the agreed design surfaced before promotion — a reviewed diff against the decision log, not a surprise found in UAT. You decide whether each deviation is a decision or a mistake.

Drift is normally invisible until it is expensive. Our pipeline refuses to promote until the decision is recorded, so the build and the documented intent cannot silently diverge.

A decision log your own engineers inherit: every architectural choice with its rationale and its consequence, written as the work happened.

Handover packs get written at the end, from memory, by whoever is left. Ours is a build artefact enforced by CI — which is what makes this asset transferable to your team instead of dependent on ours.

One application could host the showcase and every product surface behind it.
We tested

Phase 05 of 06

Validation & QA

Confirmation, not discovery. If value is first being questioned here, the framework has already failed — by this point the job is proving the build matches the evidence gathered upstream.

What this phase delivers

Simulated research panel · test generation · model evaluation
What we deliverHow S&PE differentiates

A round report scoring the build against each persona's own goals and pain points — pass, partial or fail, per goal, with the failures named. You decide which failures block a release.

Most demos are tested by the people who built them, against the flows they built. We score the build against each user's stated goals, which is how the uncomfortable failure gets found before your stakeholders find it.

An automated test suite mapped one-to-one to the acceptance criteria that were agreed, so "done" is demonstrable rather than asserted.

"It works" is a claim. A named test against a named criterion is evidence. You get a regression suite that keeps paying back long after we leave the engagement.

An observed model accuracy figure with its methodology stated beside it, measured against labelled data. You decide whether the number is fit to publish or stays internal.

The market is full of unvalidated accuracy claims. We publish the number we measured, with how we measured it — and we withdraw our own figures when the evidence does not support them. That is the standard we would want applied to a vendor pitching us.

Phase 06 of 06 · Loops back

Operations & product monitoring

Where AI stops being a build tool and becomes part of the product — and where the product starts producing the evidence the next round of discovery runs on.

What this phase delivers

Embedded classification · natural-language analytics · anomaly detection
What we deliverHow S&PE differentiates

A pre-ranked, themed and severity-scored queue waiting before anyone logs in — the triage done, so your team spends its time acting rather than sorting. You decide what the service actually works on this week.

This is where AI advisory usually stops and the client is left with a dashboard. We embed the classification in the product's daily operation, so the value recurs every morning instead of landing once in a report.

Plain-English answers over live product data, with the caveat that belongs with each trend, available on demand to people who are not analysts.

Self-service BI usually means self-service misreading. We ship the caveat with the number, so a trend cannot be quoted upward without the qualification that makes it true.

Anomalies detected against benchmark and routed straight back into the hypothesis log as new discovery items. You decide whether to investigate now, watch, or ignore.

Most delivery ends at go-live and the learning stops. Our lifecycle loops: live evidence re-enters discovery as a hypothesis, so the product keeps improving on its own operational data rather than on the next round of opinion.

How S&PE delivers

What holds across every phase

Five commitments that are not specific to one phase — they are how the engagement is run.

Streamlined

A single, prioritised plan replaces competing lists and duplicated effort. One backlog, one north star, one sequence — across strategy, discovery and delivery.

User centric

Decisions start from real user needs and validated evidence, not opinion. Every phase names the decision a human owns, and the evidence behind it is on the record.

Evidence led

AI-assisted discovery and insight generation form a data-driven foundation under the whole lifecycle, so each phase inherits evidence rather than restarting from opinion.

Scalable

Agentic AI absorbs rising data volumes and support load without proportional headcount growth, so the operating cost of the product does not track its usage curve.

Partner

Blended squads, proven playbooks and practical AI accelerators are how the capability transfers. The measure is whether the client team sustains the outcomes after we leave — which is why the decision log, the hypothesis log and the learning log are all client-owned artefacts, not ours.

Method

The honest arithmetic

The second column is what decides a live date.

AI compresses
  • Research synthesis across independent passes
  • Hypothesis generation, personas and blueprints
  • Prototype iteration — a research round becomes a build in days
  • Simulated research that kills weak ideas early
  • Code scaffolding, tests and decision-log authoring
AI does not touch
  • Data-access agreements with whoever holds the data
  • Privacy impact assessments and their sign-off
  • Security standards and penetration testing
  • Accessibility evidence, as distinct from intent
  • Deciding when confidence is sufficient — that stays with people
  • Stakeholder sign-off, two to four weeks in a regulated client

The pattern this produces

Learning time collapses; governance time does not. Planning the gates from phase 01 is the only thing that converts fast learning into an early live date — which is the argument this framework exists to make.

Named gates, not document gates

Most governance measures whether an artefact exists — business case written, requirements signed, design approved. Every phase here is bounded by a named gate instead — Discovery ready, Discover & Validate, Ready to Build, Release Ready, Value Realised — each with its own stated criteria. AI gathers the evidence; people decide when a gate's criteria are actually met.
