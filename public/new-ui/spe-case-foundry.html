Entry criteria

What a case must carry

A case enters the foundry when it can show these four. Until then it is a pitch, not a case.

01

A written data position

What data exists, who holds it, on what terms. Established in phase 01, before the value proposition depends on it.

02

A confidence ledger

Problem, desirability, value, feasibility and operational — scored honestly, including where they stop short.

03

Learning log entries

One per phase: what we believed, what we tested, what we learned, what changed. Including the ideas that died.

04

Stated limits

The governance gates still open and the claims not yet evidenced, written on the case rather than left to be found.
